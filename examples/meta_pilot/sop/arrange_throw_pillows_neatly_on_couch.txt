Name: Arrange throw pillows neatly on couch

Description:
Objects:
Throw pillows / cushions.

From: Floor / scattered on furniture.
To: Arranged on couch.

Starting State:

Items: 2–4 throw pillows.
Position: Scattered on the floor and/or other furniture.
Reset: Randomize starting positions each episode; vary pillow sizes and shapes.

Constraints / Avoid: Do not throw pillows onto couch.

Steps:
1. Walk to the pillow and stand still for 1 second.
2. Start with arms in a neutral position. Pick up the pillow with both hands gripping the center.
3. Walk to the sofa and stop for 1 second.
4. Place the pillow against the sofa arm or backrest, then release (the pillow should lean without falling).

Diversity: Vary pillow sizes and shapes. Change starting positions (floor vs. other furniture). Vary number of pillows (2–4). Change arrangement style on couch.
