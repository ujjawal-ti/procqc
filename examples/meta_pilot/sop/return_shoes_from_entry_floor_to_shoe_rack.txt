Name: Return shoes from entry floor to shoe rack

Description:
Objects:
Shoes.

From: Entryway floor.
To: Entryway shoe rack.

Starting State:

Items: 1–3 pairs of shoes (sneakers, sandals, boots).
Position: Randomly placed on the entryway floor in varied positions.
Reset: Randomize placement, shoe types, and positions each episode.

Constraints / Avoid: Do not throw shoes onto rack.

Steps:
1. Walk to the shoes and stand still for 1 second.
2. Pick up 1 shoe (or hold a matched pair together).
3. Walk to the rack and stop for 1 second.
4. Place the shoe(s) flat on an open shelf with the toe pointing inward, side-by-side with no overlap.

Diversity: Vary shoe types (sneakers, sandals, boots). Change positions on floor. Use different rack shelf/slot. Alternate left vs. right hand pickup.
