Name: Return cups and mugs from dining table to sink

Description:
Objects:
Cups, mugs.

From: Dining table.
To: Kitchen sink.

Starting State:

Items: 3–5 cups or mugs.
Position: Random positions on the dining table.
Reset: Vary placement and orientation each episode.

Constraints / Avoid: None specified.

Steps:
1. Walk to the table and stand still for 1 second.
2. Start with arms in a neutral position. Pick up 1 cup by the handle.
3. Walk to the sink and stop for 1 second.
4. Place the cup upright inside the sink basin. Return arms to a neutral position.
5. Repeat for remaining cups (1 at a time).

Diversity: Mix up coffee mugs, ceramic cups, plastic cups, with/without handles. Vary positions across the table. Change handle orientation each time. Switch between left- and right-handed pickups.
