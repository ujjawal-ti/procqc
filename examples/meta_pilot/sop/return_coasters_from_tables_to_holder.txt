Name: Return coasters from tables to holder

Description:
Objects:
Coasters.

From: Scattered on coffee/side tables.
To: Coaster holder / stacked neatly.

Starting State:

Items: 3-5 coasters.
Position: Scattered across different tables (coffee table, side tables).
Reset: Vary coaster materials and positions each episode.

Constraints / Avoid: Do not slide coasters off table edges. Do not stack carelessly (align neatly).

Steps:
1. Walk to the table and stand still for 1 second.
2. Start with arms in a neutral position. Pick up 1 coaster at a time using a thumb-and-forefinger pinch grip across opposite ends.
3. (If walking, otherwise skip if stationary) Walk to the holder or stack spot and stop for 1 second.
4. Place the coaster flat on the holder/stack with edges aligned to the coaster underneath.
5. Gently nudge the coaster into place to adjust alignment.

Diversity: Vary coaster materials (wood, cork, stone). Change positions across different tables (coffee table, side tables). Vary number of coasters (2–4).
