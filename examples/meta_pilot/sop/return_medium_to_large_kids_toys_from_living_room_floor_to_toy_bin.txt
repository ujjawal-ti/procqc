Name: Return medium to large kids toys from living room floor to toy bin

Description:
Objects:
Kids toys (blocks, cars, plush toys, etc.) (avoid small parts or items that require fine manipulation).

From: Floor / sofa.
To: Toy bin.

Starting State:

Items: 3–5 larger kids toys (blocks, cars, plush toys).
Position: Scattered on the floor and sofa.
Reset: Randomize positions each episode.

Constraints / Avoid: Prohibited Objects: Small parts or items requiring fine manipulation. Prohibited Actions: Do not throw toys. Do not overfill bin.

Steps:
1. Walk to the toy and stand still for 1 second.
2. Start with arms in a neutral position. Pick up 1 large toy with both hands, or gather 2–3 small ones in one hand.
3. Walk to the bin and stop for 1 second.
4. Hold item(s) over the bin opening and release.

Diversity: Vary toy types (blocks, cars, plush). Change positions (floor vs. sofa). Vary number of items (3–5).
