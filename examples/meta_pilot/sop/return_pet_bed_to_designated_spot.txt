Name: Return pet bed to designated spot

Description:
Objects:
Pet bed.

From: Sofa / floor.
To: Designated pet bed spot in the living room.

Starting State:

Items: 1 pet bed.
Position: On the sofa or a random floor location away from its designated spot.
Reset: Vary starting position and orientation each episode.

Constraints / Avoid: None specified.

Steps:
1. Walk to the pet bed and stand still for 1 second.
2. Start with arms in a neutral position. Pick up the bed with both hands gripping opposite edges.
3. Walk to the designated floor spot and stop for 1 second.
4. Lower the bed flat onto the floor so it rests without folding.

Diversity: Vary pet bed size. Change starting position (sofa vs. random floor location). Vary orientation. Use one-hand vs. two-hand grip based on bed size.
