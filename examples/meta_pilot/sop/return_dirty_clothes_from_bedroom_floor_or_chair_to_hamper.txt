Name: Return dirty clothes from bedroom floor or chair to hamper

Description:
Objects:
Clothing.

From: Bedroom floor / chair.
To: Laundry hamper in bedroom/closet.

Starting State:

Items: ~5-10 clothing items.
Position: On the floor and/or draped on a chair.
Reset: Randomize item positions and types each episode. Vary hamper location (bedroom vs. closet).

Constraints / Avoid: Do not overstuff hamper. Do not knock over furniture.

Steps:
1. Walk to the clothes and stand still for 1 second.
2. Start with arms in a neutral position. Pick up 1–2 items with one hand using a full-palm grasp.
3. Walk to the hamper and stop for 1 second.
4. Hold items over the hamper opening and release them inside.

Diversity: Vary item types (shirts, pants, socks). Vary positions (floor vs. chair vs. bed edge). Vary number of items (1–3). Vary hamper location (bedroom vs. closet).
