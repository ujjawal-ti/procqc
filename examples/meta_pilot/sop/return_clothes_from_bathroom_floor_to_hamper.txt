Name: Return clothes from bathroom floor to hamper

Description:
Objects:
Clothes.

From: Bathroom floor / behind door.
To: Laundry hamper in bathroom/hallway.

Starting State:

Items: 1–3 clothing items (towels, shirts, socks).
Position: On the floor or draped behind the door in random positions.
Reset: Randomize positions and item types each episode.

Constraints / Avoid: Prohibited Objects: No wet or soiled items. Prohibited Actions: Do not overstuff hamper. Do not drag items on floor.

Steps:
1. Walk to the clothes and stand still for 1 second.
2. Start with arms in a neutral position. Pick up 1–2 items with one hand using a full-palm grasp.
3. Walk to the hamper and stop for 1 second.
4. Hold items over the hamper opening and release them inside.

Diversity: Vary item types (towels, shirts, socks). Change positions (floor vs. behind door). Vary the number of items (1–3). Change hamper location (bathroom vs. hallway).
