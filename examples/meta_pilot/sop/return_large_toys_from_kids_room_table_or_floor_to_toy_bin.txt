Name: Return large toys from kids room table or floor to toy bin

Description:
Objects:
Toys (blocks, cars, dolls, plushies, etc.).

From: Kids room floor.
To: Toy bin / basket.

Starting State:

Items: 3–6 toys.
Position: Randomly scattered on the floor and/or table.
Reset: Randomize positions each episode.

Constraints / Avoid: Prohibited Objects: No small parts or items requiring fine manipulation. Prohibited Actions: Do not throw toys into bin. Do not overfill bin.

Steps:
1. Walk to the toys and stand still for 1 second.
2. Start with arms in a neutral position. Pick up 1 large toy with both hands, or gather 2–3 small items (e.g., blocks) in one cupped hand.
3. Walk to the bin and stop for 1 second.
4. Hold the item(s) directly over the bin opening and place into the bin.
5. Repeat until the floor is clear.

Diversity: Vary toy types (blocks, cars, dolls, plushies). Change positions (floor vs. table). Vary number of items (3–6). Change bin location.
