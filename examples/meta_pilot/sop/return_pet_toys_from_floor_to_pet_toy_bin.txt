Name: Return pet toys from floor to pet toy bin

Description:
Objects:
Pet toys (plush toys, rubber balls, rope toys, etc.).

From: Floor.
To: Pet toy bin/basket.

Starting State:

Items: 2–4 pet toys (plush, rubber, rope, ball).
Position: Scattered on the floor in random positions.
Reset: Randomize positions and vary toy types each episode.

Constraints / Avoid: Do not throw toys into bin.

Steps:
1. Walk to the toy and stand still for 1 second.
2. Start with arms in a neutral position. Pick up the toy with one hand, wrapping your fingers around the widest part.
3. Walk to the bin and stop for 1 second.
4. Hold the toy over the bin opening and release.

Diversity: Vary toy types (plush, rubber, rope). Change positions on floor. Vary number of items (2–4). Change bin location.
